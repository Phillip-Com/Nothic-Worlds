# Plants

Low-poly models of trees, bushes, small plants, and stones for the standing view (VISION.md
`REN-06`; `Rendering/PlantModels.cs`). All are **CC0 1.0** (public domain): free for any use,
including commercial, with no attribution required. They are credited here anyway.

| Files | Source | Author | Link |
|-------|--------|--------|------|
| All `*.obj` and `*.mtl` | Ultimate Nature Pack (OBJ versions, unchanged) | Quaternius | https://quaternius.itch.io/150-lowpoly-nature-models |

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (the pack's `License.txt`).

Only some of the pack's 150 models are kept (2.3 MB). Each model's materials are flat colors,
merged into one mesh colored by its vertices when the standing view loads them. To swap one,
keep its file name, its base at height 0, and its up along +Y; its size doesn't matter (plants
are scaled to their heights in `PlantMix`).
